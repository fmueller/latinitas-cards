## Nominal Lexical Forms

FR-1, high. Setup can propose and confirm nominal roles for the same source note
type as verbs, with explicit word-class evidence from configured tags (including
`Substantiv` / `Adjektiv`) or a confirmed field. Mixed profiles must route each entry
unambiguously. Missing or conflicting evidence withholds the entry; shape alone
never determines word class.

Nouns support nominative singular, genitive singular and gender m./f./n./c., with
annotations for irregular genitive plural and plural-only usage. Plural-only forms
such as `castra, castrōrum n. (Pl.)` must use plural roles or explicit number-aware
roles; never label them singular. Adjectives cover three-termination forms including
`aliēnus, aliēna, aliēnum` and `ācer, ācris, ācre`, two-termination `fortis, forte`,
and one-termination `fēlīx, fēlīcis`, without inventing missing source forms.

Provide nominal completion and recognition recipes with German user terminology
(Nominativ, Genitiv, Genus, maskulin/feminin/neutrum and common gender). Version
their note-type/template contract without renumbering verb ordinals. Preview shows
eligible, withheld and skipped nominal coverage separately from verb coverage.

Acceptance: a confirmed mixed note-type fixture produces correct noun/adjective
and unchanged verb cards; plural-only roles are truthful; an untagged look-alike and
conflicting tags stay withheld. Native import/update preserves existing verb cards
and scheduling while adding nominal cards through an explicit setup/migration.

## Evidence Proposals And Reviewed Calibration

FR-2, high. Produce proposed segmentation, explanation and form-appropriate
translation from a versioned, citable bundled or user-supplied morphology/lexicon
source. Record source locator, version/checksum, applicable pattern, proposed value
and evidence binding. Reference coverage and licensing must be established before
bundling; unsupported claims retain no fabricated value. Proposals are not acceptance.

A reviewer-confirmed sample may authorize reuse for a precisely bounded regular
pattern, such as regular a-conjugation, under a recorded calibration decision.
Define sample selection, independent held-out checks and acceptance criteria before
consumers. Record reviewer, sample decisions, pattern scope, supported claim classes,
reference/analyzer versions and exclusions. Each accepted claim links to the exact
calibration and passes its applicability/evidence checks. Sample approval alone does
not attest arbitrary lexemes, irregular verbs or semantic translations.

Acceptance: a supported regular a-conjugation fixture gains accepted label,
segmentation and explanation without entering each claim value manually. Contrasting
irregular, out-of-scope, contradictory and reference-missing fixtures remain withheld.
Form translation requires its own applicable evidence and decision. No claim in
this route becomes accepted without an individual reviewer decision or an explicit
recorded calibration; preserve separate human and policy/calibration provenance.
Changing source, pattern, reference or calibration policy invalidates affected reuse;
`claims inspect --check` fails closed. Integrate with v0.5.0 decisions/adapters and
whole-assertion checks after T-186 approval, without weakening their acceptance model.
Calibration/reference drift or withdrawal also uses v0.5.0 export receipts and the
installed-content ineligibility handoff. List affected installed semantic identities
and required manual or supported managed resolution. Omitting later CSV rows does
not update installed assertions or acceptance status. The integration owner installs
a calibration-accepted card, withdraws its calibration/reference, regenerates and
exercises that resolution, preserving history and scheduling without asserting stale
acceptance. Consume T-195 receipts/handoff rather than create a second mechanism.

The contract-design checkpoint must choose and license an actual reference or define
a supported user-supplied schema. It must define measured calibration criteria and
independently judged examples. If usable evidence is unavailable or calibration fails,
report unsupported/not evaluable and block this outcome, not lower the standard.

## Individual Review Round Trip

FR-3, high. Add `claims export-review` and `claims import-review`. CSV is the proposed
editable interchange; the design checkpoint freezes its versioned schema and encoding.
Export lemma, form, proposed role/value, evidence text and fingerprint, with empty
decision/reviewer/reason cells. Import accepts only explicit accept/reject decisions
with a reviewer; reason is optional. Blank decisions stay open. There is no approve-all.
Treat cells as untrusted data, escape spreadsheet-formula prefixes without changing
canonical bindings, and never infer acceptance from order or an edited proposal.
The schema defines accept/reject mapping to internal accepted/withheld decisions
and a deterministic audit reason when the optional human reason is empty.

Acceptance: mark 10 rows accept, 2 reject and leave the remainder blank; import
records exactly 12 individual decisions. Reject stale/unknown fingerprints, changed
bound evidence, duplicate/conflicting decisions, invalid verdicts or missing reviewers
with row diagnostics and no partial writes. Reimporting an identical decided file is
idempotent. Preserve existing batch validation and source/calibration drift checks.

Attesting a genuine PPP label unlocks its eligible PPP cards. Supine-only fourth
forms may retain their existing supine cards but must not unlock PPP cards. Missing
`-` and future-participle `-ūrus` fixtures must not be relabeled as PPP by this import.
The review sheet exposes enough source evidence to make that individual distinction.

## Verb Parsing And Skip Reports

FR-4, high. Profile-confirmed normalization can separate a principal-parts sequence
from trailing HTML usage blocks, distinguish compound-boundary `|` from pipe
alternatives, and represent `a / b` as alternatives within one role. Preserve source
bytes/provenance; retain compound markers for display but ignore them for matching.
Do not remove meaningful inline material or guess ambiguous truncation boundaries.
Alternative forms are evidence, not blanket approval of every alternative or label.

Extend confirmed layouts/role semantics for deponents and semi-deponents, including
passive present `hortor`, infinitive `hortārī` and perfect `hortātus sum`; do not
classify grammatical voice from a suffix alone or invent absent active forms.
Resolved parsing still passes linguistic review/acceptance before export.

Provide an exhaustive skip report in `preview --json` and a readable table with
source key, lemma, reason code and offending fragment; mark display truncation and
offer complete output. Separate parse success from eligible-card counts.

Acceptance: fixtures for `timēre, timeō, timuī, -` followed by an HTML usage block,
`ad|esse`, `placēre, placeō, placuī / placitus sum, placitum`, and a deponent produce
cards or a specific documented withholding reason. Check `esse<div>(est – sunt)</div>`,
non-breaking spaces, absent roles and a semi-deponent. The nominal `prae|ceptum`
must not become a verb through normalization. Conflicting pipe policies and genuinely
ambiguous usage boundaries fail closed; source checksums remain unchanged.
Even when later linguistic review withholds a card, assert the expected parsed role
sequence, preserved alternatives and separated usage text for each supported fixture;
an implementation that merely skips every difficult entry does not pass.

## Stable Checkpoint Location

FR-5, medium. Bind checkpoint lookup to confirmed source identity and stable profile
configuration, not the dated package filename. Design freezes a profile-relative
default or state directory plus explicit `--checkpoint PATH` override and precedence.
Keep existing CSV identity-state behavior separate. A profile path alone is not
proof that two packages are the same source collection.
The contract gate must define a confirmed source-stream binding and its authority:
how later exports establish continuity, and how uncertain/conflicting bindings fail
closed. Do not assume package metadata or overlapping note GUIDs intrinsically prove
collection identity. Define discovery/migration or explicit reattachment of existing
filename-adjacent review state through approved v0.5.0 adapters, never a second store
or automatic reattestation of changed claims.

Support explicit, validated migration from input-adjacent checkpoints. Conflicting
legacy/stable files, source-identity mismatch and corrupt/stale data must not be
silently merged, overwritten or bypassed through fresh-import approval. Preserve the
old checkpoint until the new write is successful; define interruption recovery.
Errors name the effective expected path and safe recovery procedure.

Acceptance: generate from `collection-A.colpkg`, then a later same-source
`collection-B.colpkg`; prior-export evidence is found without an approval flag.
Generated identities and retained evidence are stable; a genuinely different
collection cannot reuse the checkpoint. Test explicit override, migration conflict,
missing state and interrupted write. Do not claim the checkpoint proves destination
import, scheduling continuity or permission for lifecycle changes in Anki.
Review a PPP label and another claim on A; unchanged relevant content on B retains
those valid decisions and card eligibility. Changed content invalidates affected
decisions without discarding unrelated valid decisions. A different source containing
overlapping note GUIDs cannot inherit A's state from that overlap alone.
