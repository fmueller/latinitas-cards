## Nominal Lexical Forms

FR-1, high. Setup can propose and confirm nominal roles for the same source note
type as verbs, with explicit word-class evidence from configured tags (including
`Substantiv` / `Adjektiv`) or a confirmed field. Mixed profiles must route each entry
unambiguously. Missing or conflicting evidence withholds the entry; shape alone
never determines word class.

Nouns support nominative singular, genitive singular and gender m./f./n./c., with
annotations for irregular genitive plural and plural-only usage. Plural-only forms
such as `castra, castrōrum n. (Pl.)` must use plural roles or explicit number-aware
roles; never label them singular. Adjectives cover three-termination forms including
`aliēnus, aliēna, aliēnum` and `ācer, ācris, ācre`, two-termination `fortis, forte`,
and one-termination `fēlīx, fēlīcis`, without inventing missing source forms.

Provide nominal completion and recognition recipes with German user terminology
(Nominativ, Genitiv, Genus, maskulin/feminin/neutrum and common gender). Version
their note-type/template contract without renumbering verb ordinals. Preview shows
eligible, withheld and skipped nominal coverage separately from verb coverage.

Acceptance: a confirmed mixed note-type fixture produces correct noun/adjective
and unchanged verb cards; plural-only roles are truthful; an untagged look-alike and
conflicting tags stay withheld. Native import/update preserves existing verb cards
and scheduling while adding nominal cards through an explicit setup/migration.

## Individual Review Round Trip

Treat cells as untrusted data, escape spreadsheet-formula prefixes without changing

The schema defines accept/reject mapping to internal accepted/withheld decisions

records exactly 12 individual decisions. Reject stale/unknown fingerprints, changed

FR-4, high. Approved expanded report and acceptance amendment. The later run used lexical
field `Latein`, principal-parts field `Konstruktion_Hinweise`, comma-separated
infinitive/present/perfect/PPP roles, German terminology and both recipes. It reports
215 `Verb`-tagged notes, 115 producing cards and 100 skipped. This differs from the
earlier report's 123 generated notes; neither run was reproduced and their exact
inputs/configurations are not available to establish why. Do not combine their counts.

| Reported v0.4.0 primary skip code | Distinct skipped notes | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `multi_object_source` | 65 | Compound pipes and lexical annotations are recovery targets |
| `unmarked_omission` | 24 | Three-part deponents/semi-deponents are recovery targets; defective entries still require explicit treatment |
| `extra_separator` | 2 | Usage annotation/alternative parsing needs improvement |
| `too_few_parts` | 6 | Impersonal/defective entries stay excluded with existing codes |
| `missing_principal_parts` | 2 | Empty fields stay excluded |
| `separator_mismatch` | 1 | Mis-tagged nominal entry stays excluded, not guessed into a verb |

Profile-confirmed normalization applies to both lexical and principal-parts fields:
separate trailing HTML blocks and recognized plain-text parenthesized usage notes,
`+ Dat.`, `+ Abl.`, `mit Akk.` and `X = Y` usage lines from lexical forms. Retain
raw evidence and stripped fragments with rule provenance; never mutate the source.
Do not strip grammatical distinctions such as `(Perf.)`, alternative forms such as
`coeptum (in|ceptum)`, or number/gender annotations merely because they are parenthesized.
Unconfirmed or conflicting boundaries fail closed rather than greedily truncate text.

Treat internal `|` as a compound marker under a separate confirmed profile option
from `pipe_alternatives`. Preserve it for card display; ignore it in canonical lexical
and answer-equivalence matching and never split it into objects or alternatives.
The T-206 contract must define display versus canonical-match representations and
verify the actual answer surface. Current reference cards reveal answers, without a
typed-answer grader; this requirement does not imply one already exists or authorize
a new typing/grading UI. If typed grading is needed, resolve it as an explicit design
decision before consumers. Preserve current matching behavior for unchanged profiles.

Extend confirmed layouts/role semantics for deponents and semi-deponents, including
`loquī, loquor, locūtus sum`, `audēre, audeō, ausus sum` and `gaudēre, gaudeō,
gāvīsus sum (+ Abl.)`. Distinguish passive-form present and compound perfect roles
and supply German deponent/semi-deponent labels. Do not classify voice from suffix
alone, infer a PPP from a three-part count, or invent absent active forms. Represent
`placuī / placitus sum` as alternatives of one perfect role, with their grammatical
distinctions retained. Alternatives are evidence, not blanket linguistic acceptance.

Provide exhaustive `preview --json` and a complete readable table, plus CSV export,
with source note key, lemma, primary skip code, related reasons, offending fragments
and class/scope provenance. Define deterministic note-level aggregation separately
from per-entry/per-card diagnostics: a partially productive note is not counted as
fully skipped. Do not count each recipe/role as a separate skipped source note.
Support the confirmed `Verb` scope without treating its tags as unquestionable proof
of grammatical class. Complete report modes have no hidden truncation; bounded
interactive summaries may point to them and label omissions.
