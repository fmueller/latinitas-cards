# LatinitasCards v0.5.5

## Summary

Planned, inactive release after v0.5.1 and before v0.6.0. Adds sentence-function and
construction exercises from original Latin corpus passages, using v0.5.0's shared library
and LLM verification, automatic acceptance, CLI orchestration, reports and Anki status.
Support both finding corpus examples for source-deck vocabulary and creating exercises
from selected readings. Translation remains primary; contextual grammar explains how the
text works rather than requiring the learner to proofread generated claims.

This release discovers structure in supplied passages. v0.5.0 already owns isolated forms
and short, controlled combinations whose structure is specified by supported patterns;
v0.5.1 adds nominal lexical cards. Complete the morphology coverage below through those
shared recipes and adapters, not a second form or acceptance engine. Corpus syntax discovery
remains distinct from controlled generation; the boundary is not the number of words on a
card. Do not move v0.5.0's curated grammar/mnemonic intake into this release.

## Planning Status And Dependencies

The entry points, selection behavior and presentation record user decisions. Items marked
**proposed technical contract** are recommendations requiring explicit design approval,
not approved wire formats or a claim of implemented coverage. v0.5.5 and v0.6.0 remain
inactive; v0.5.0 remains active and v0.5.1's approved planning package is unchanged.
This document neither activates a spec nor authorizes implementation.

Depend on v0.4.0's normalized corpora, citation/span preservation, corpus exercise identity
and file-based handoff, then v0.5.0's versioned evidence/acceptance and provider pipeline.
Add explicit adapters/schema upgrades where needed: existing strict morphology-only models
are not assumed to accept syntax roles, construction spans or automatic acceptance records.
Existing accepted corpus exercises remain usable without enabling a model.
Depend on v0.5.1's approved nominal role/template and reference/calibration contracts before
extending nominal recipes. Its planning approval is not approval of those future contracts.

## Goals

- Practice Latin-to-German translation using actual text, with form and construction
  recognition as supporting exercises; retain explicit German/English generated content.
- Find useful passages for known deck entries and support passage-first study without a deck.
- Discover supported constructions automatically, subject to profile/CLI filters and budgets.
- Explain unsupported material and lower accepted-card yield instead of silently omitting it.
- Extend automatic checking without claiming exhaustive or error-free Latin syntax analysis.
- Give nouns and adjectives the same evidence-backed analysis, exercise and reporting support
  as verbs, with explicit paradigm coverage rather than verb-only acceptance examples.

## Potential Features

### Complete Morphology Coverage

The required baseline is standard Classical and biblical Latin morphology, evaluated
separately on both kinds of material. "Complete" means implementing and independently
testing every applicable entry in an approved, finite inventory bound to the following
matrix and versioned, citable references. The inventory requires approval before
implementation and identifies paradigm patterns, licensed lexical exceptions and variants,
feature combinations and recipe applicability. It does not require every Latin lexeme,
historical spelling or theoretical
combination. Applicability is determined independently of implementation: missing evidence,
unsupported capability and untested capability do not establish linguistic nonexistence.
References, analyzers and LLM agreement are evidence inputs, not automatic acceptance.

| Family | Required morphology and distinctions |
| --- | --- |
| Nouns | All five declensions; third-declension consonant, i-stem and mixed patterns; masculine, feminine, neuter and lexical common gender; singular/plural; nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, ablative and vocative; attested locatives where applicable. Include neuter rules, gender exceptions, plural-only/singular-only and indeclinable nouns, and reference-attested irregular, heteroclitic and Greek-derived patterns. |
| Adjectives | First/second-declension adjectives and third-declension one-, two- and three-termination patterns; all applicable masculine/feminine/neuter, case and number combinations; pronominal adjective inflection; positive, comparative and superlative forms, including regular, irregular/suppletive and periphrastic comparison where licensed. Distinguish adjectives without particular comparison degrees from missing evidence. |
| Agreement and related forms | Attributive and predicative adjective agreement, including nonmatching surface endings and common-gender nouns resolved by context; substantivized adjectives; participles and gerundives with nominal agreement while retaining their verbal roles. Personal, reflexive, possessive, demonstrative, relative, interrogative and indefinite pronouns, and declinable numerals, must have an explicit reference-backed paradigm inventory rather than silently falling back to noun rules. |
| Verbs | Preserve principal-part and PPP contracts; cover the four conjugations and mixed third-conjugation -io patterns, applicable person/number, tense, mood and voice combinations, deponent/semideponent, irregular, defective and impersonal verbs. Include reference-licensed infinitives, participles, gerund, gerundive, supine and periphrastic forms; never fill nonexistent cells by mechanically crossing feature lists. |
| Invariable words | Recognize indeclinable categories and comparative/superlative adverbs where licensed. Do not assign case, gender, conjugation or other inapplicable features merely to fill the matrix. |

The verb inventory distinguishes present and perfect systems, licensed indicative and
subjunctive tenses, present/future imperatives, infinitive tense/voice alternatives and
compound-form agreement. Record inflectional shape, lexical voice class and contextual
meaning separately. Passive-shaped inflection does not automatically imply passive meaning;
deponent classification must not exclude licensed active-form nonfinite forms or determine
participle/gerundive meaning without applicable evidence.

Defective and impersonal coverage applies through explicitly upgraded capabilities;
v0.5.1's existing-profile exclusions remain unchanged. Licensed paradigms retain their actual
gaps and uses, including the distinction between morphological tense and temporal meaning.
Expanded coverage must not pad old entries into full paradigms or silently change their
unchanged-policy results.

Separate lexical properties from occurrence-level features. A noun's common-gender lexical
classification is not a fourth adjective agreement ending. Declension/conjugation classes
are not inferred from one surface ending. Syncretic forms retain all defensible readings
unless the supplied context resolves them; gender is grammatical, not inferred from a
translation or a person's identity. Record attested alternatives and orthographic variants
without conflating distinct lemmas, senses or source spans.

Each applicable matrix cell must work through analysis, optional recognition, translation
and opt-in production/form clozes where the prompt and lexical evidence support that recipe.
Nominal lexical completion continues to use v0.5.1 roles; contextual analysis and controlled
form exercises share the expanded morphology contract. Default translation-first selection,
no implicit output-card quota and duplicate suppression remain unchanged. Coverage is a
capability requirement, not a requirement to emit every cell as a card. A corpus-only
example is not proof that controlled generation or nominal parsing supports the same form.

Preserve v0.5.0's saved grammar-study preset, bilingual behavior and independent recipe
selection. Complete morphology coverage does not require a curated rule, component explanation
or mnemonic for every paradigm cell. Curated recipes retain their applicable item and
rendered-card human approvals; missing curated content does not invalidate otherwise
supported form exercises or authorize invented teaching content.

Preview and reports expose coverage by word class, paradigm, feature combination and recipe,
separately for deck-driven and reading-driven paths. Distinguish implemented/tested coverage
from accepted-output correctness, completion on evidence-sufficient input and yield on
selected material. Report not applicable, missing lexical/reference
evidence, unsupported capability, ambiguous/withheld, failed and deferred outcomes without
counting them as successful coverage. Known defective or invariable cells are not failures;
absence of evidence is not proof that a form cannot exist. Preserve the shared report
taxonomy and its accepted/selected versus accepted-but-unselected distinction.

Acceptance requires an independently judged, reference-cited coverage inventory and held-out
cases per family, with expected analyses/forms and licensed variants. Each applicable
capability entry requires independently specified, evidence-sufficient positive cases that
produce the expected supported outcomes and usable exercises where the recipe applies.
Withheld, unsupported, failed and untested outcomes do not satisfy positive coverage;
correct withholding is required safety behavior, not a substitute for demonstrated support.
Test every applicable paradigm cell against that inventory, including
all six ordinary cases in both numbers for each regular noun pattern and each applicable
adjective gender/degree, and separate attested locative/exception cases. Use contrasting
controls such as masculine first-declension nouns, neuter accusatives, `poēta bonus`
(agreement without matching endings), `fortis/forte`, `fēlīx/fēlīcis`, `bonus/melior/optimus`,
and `castra` without invented singulars. An isolated `puellae` retains its defensible
readings; contextual disambiguation must be checked independently rather than assumed.
Cover pronoun/numeral irregularity, participial agreement and deponent/defective verb controls.
Missing stems, ambiguous gender, invalid degree formation and unsupported variant controls
must not yield fabricated accepted forms. Check both correct output and expected withholding,
not just successful import. Run recipe, coverage-report and no-answer-leakage journeys in
German and English. Independent correctness, evidence-sufficient completion and coverage
release thresholds and evaluation groups require approval before consumer implementation.
Full-coverage claims require passing every applicable inventory entry's capability checks
and the held-out thresholds for each required group, with results separated by family,
applicable recipe and Classical/biblical material. Aggregate success cannot conceal a failing
required group; implementation gaps in the baseline block full-coverage claims.
Individual uncertain lexemes remain withheld, not invented to meet a threshold.
Changing the baseline requires an explicit maintainer scope decision and a
revised spec, not a silent narrowing by an analyzer or evaluation owner.

**Proposed technical contract:** define versioned lexical/paradigm metadata,
occurrence analyses, alternatives, recipe applicability and coverage records, with explicit
adapters for existing strict schemas. In particular, `latinitas-morph/1` and reviewed form
parsing are not assumed to accept new fields or categories. Preserve old payloads, identities,
template ordinals and unchanged German output; any new schema/template requires an explicit
upgrade. Evidence or paradigm-policy changes invalidate dependent accepted claims through
the shared pipeline. Presentation changes alone do not invalidate linguistic acceptance.

### Deck-Driven Corpus Examples

Given a confirmed source deck/profile and selected normalized corpora, discover occurrences
of selected vocabulary and propose contextual exercises. Match inflected forms using
supported analysis with explicit lexical/sense evidence, not surface-string equality alone.
Deck knowledge helps prioritize appropriate passages; it does not prove a passage's syntax
or that every occurrence has the deck entry's sense. Preserve alternatives and explain
unresolved homographs, sense conflicts and missing matches.

Use original wording with source edition/work/native citation and enough sentence/passage
context to support the question. Do not invent an example and label it as Vulgate or another
source. If a passage is shortened, retain its source span and expose the full context on
the answer/report; do not silently turn fragments into altered quotations.

Acceptance: link a deck entry to an inflected occurrence, distinguish a homograph/wrong-sense
control, retain exact source text and citation, and report no suitable match without
fabricating a passage. The source deck is read-only; a corpus link never changes deck identity.

### Reading-Driven Exercises

Given selected works/passages or reading ranges, discover eligible grammar targets without
requiring a source deck. An optional deck supplies vocabulary priorities and difficulty
signals, not a prerequisite for source identity or grammatical acceptance. Both entry points
use the same proposal, evidence, selection and export contracts. Reaching the same semantic
exercise through both paths must not create duplicate notes or reset existing schedules.

Translation cards show the Latin before its analysis. Recognition cards identify a target
without revealing the tested function; answers explain its contribution to meaning and
include a reference translation only when that translation is sufficiently supported.
Reference wording is guidance, not exact-string grading. A construction explanation or
translation is its own checked assertion, not automatically approved by accepted morphology.

Default translation units are manageable original sentences or coherent parts of longer
sentences. Keep a short sentence whole; for a longer sentence, neutrally mark only the
section to translate and keep the context needed to interpret it visible on the front.
The answer/report exposes the full source context and citation. Do not reveal the tested
construction with a front-side label such as "Translate the purpose clause". An AcI or
another dependent construction must retain the governing expression and other context
needed for a supported reading; do not split at arbitrary word or length boundaries.
Do not automatically generate both a whole-sentence card and cards for every component.
Multiple units from one passage need distinct learning objectives rather than quota-filling
variants. Optional recognition remains supporting, not a mandatory sibling for every unit.

Acceptance: a selected passage produces supported exercises without a deck; adding deck
priorities affects selection rather than source identity. Both entry points can converge
on one exercise. Ambiguous or incomplete context retains defensible alternatives or withholds
the question; missing translation support cannot silently produce an asserted translation.
Demonstrate a long sentence with a main clause, relative clause and purpose clause as
separate possible translation targets: each front preserves needed context without naming
the tested role, and each answer translates only its marked target with relevant supporting
analysis. Keep a short sentence intact and reject a split that removes necessary AcI context.
Run these presentation/meaning controls for both German and English output.

### Supported Construction Library And Automatic Discovery

Implement and test a bounded library covering basic subject/predicate/object/attribute and
prepositional functions, plus AcI, ablative absolute, participium coniunctum and selected
subordinate-clause types. The target includes relative clauses, purpose/result clauses with
ut/ne as appropriate, cum clauses, indirect questions, conditional clauses and other causal,
temporal and concessive clauses. This broader examination-oriented target does not promise
every subtype or occurrence in arbitrary Latin. Exact supported subtypes, annotation
conventions and negative boundaries require design approval, with independent evaluation for each
supported subgroup. If reliability is insufficient, narrow or disable that automatic path
and report the unsupported or withheld outcomes; do not lower the acceptance standard to
claim coverage. The broader target remains visible even where initial support is limited.

Within that library, discover candidates from the supplied deck/corpora automatically.
Profiles and CLI filters restrict construction types, vocabulary, passages, difficulty
and card count. Do not require selection of each candidate. By default, process the full
selected material within configured processing limits and export all eligible cards after
duplicate/redundant-variant suppression, without a fixed default output-card quota. Reuse
v0.5.0's optional smaller export batches and stored-result continuation. Respect previous
selections and suppression as applicable. Report selection factors, limits and deferred
processing; do not silently replace v0.4.0's ranking rule or use raw model confidence as a
new ranking factor. Prefer fewer supported cards to including uncertain cards in Anki.

A user filter narrows the supported set; it does not authorize an unsupported analysis.
Report detected unsupported constructions and explicit failures where detectable. Do not
claim every unsupported construction was discovered or classify all unknown text as
unsupported. No fixed corpus/course whitelist: process any supported input, report the
demonstrated evaluation scope, and withhold unresolved candidates individually.

Acceptance: profile and CLI restrictions exclude the requested categories deterministically,
processing limits bound analysis without silently imposing an output-card quota, and
near-duplicates do not inflate the selection. With enough processing budget, a selected
reading with more than 20 eligible cards exports them without requiring repeated runs.
For each supported family, include a valid case, an ambiguity/conflicting-evidence case and
a documented unsupported boundary. Test classical and biblical material separately and
publish actual coverage rather than infer it from a successful import.

### Contextual Verification And Material-Yield Reports

Extend v0.5.0's evidence and blind counter-check pipeline with passage context, target and
construction spans, sense choices, dependency/role alternatives and conventions. A dependency
parse alone does not prove a pedagogical sentence function. A blind check must not receive
the first model's labels or explanations disguised as source facts. Upstream accepted
morphology may be supplied with its provenance; proposed syntax remains the task to solve.

Automatically accept only complete supported assertions under the applicable policy with
no unresolved objections. The same model may be used for separate proposal/check runs;
second-model support is configurable, not mandatory. Do not equate agreement or high model
confidence with human review or measured correctness. Shared invalidation/reuse rules bind
the actual passage, relevant annotations, source/deck links, check policy and model/prompt
configuration. Changed passage context invalidates its dependent analyses.

Use the shared optional review queue and report taxonomy: not generated, not processed/deferred,
withheld, processing failed, accepted but not selected, and accepted/selected. Summarize by
material and exercise type, with source-linked examples and reasons. Distinguish a lack of
suitable passages or supported constructions from model outages, linguistic conflicts,
processing limits and output-card selection budgets. Do not invent candidate counts for
unprocessed input. No need to approve every withheld item before using supported output.

Acceptance: a new passage can yield automatically accepted cards without per-claim human
approval. Unresolved model disagreement, insufficient context and a model outage produce
different report outcomes. Passage edits invalidate dependent checks while unrelated
validated work can be reused. Reuse the shared evaluation protocol with independent syntax
judgments; permit negative or not-evaluable results and narrow the affected acceptance path.
Exhaust processing limits before the whole reading is analyzed, distinguish deferred work
from accepted-but-unselected exercises, and resume without repeating validated checks.

### Syntax Presentation And Anki Handoff

Add optional independent semantic classes for sentence functions and construction spans,
building on v0.5.0 morphology themes. Support configurable course conventions, light/dark
appearance and non-color cues. Explanatory colors/labels default to the answer; the front
may neutrally mark the target without leaking its tested role. Preserve original text and
handle nested/overlapping constructions without corrupting passage spans or HTML.

Reuse the small answer-side acceptance status and collapsible `Warum diese Antwort?`
section. Explain the construction-specific basis and show source context/citation; keep
detailed stage results in the report and relevant limitations in the evidence section.
Do not show uncalibrated model-confidence percentages on cards or in tags. Note tags
summarize acceptance conservatively; precise per-card status stays inspectable. Fully checked
automatic cards are not relabeled as human reviewed. Theme changes do not affect linguistic
acceptance, identities or schedules.

**Proposed technical contract:** define versioned syntax/span annotations, exercise semantic
keys, layout/field ownership and explicit note-type/template migration before implementation. Reuse
existing corpus layouts where compatible; do not silently repurpose frozen slots or promise
that a new recipe needs no schema change. File export and managed updates remain canonical;
omitting an old card from new output does not delete or safely retire its existing Anki card.
Direct Anki provisioning/application remains v0.6.0 scope.

Acceptance: inspect supported exercises in Linux Anki Desktop in German and English,
light/dark modes, and collapsed/expanded evidence states. Check neutral fronts, nested
markings, keyboard accessibility, import/update identity and scheduling, user fields/tags,
and explicit handling of previously accepted exercises that become ineligible.

## Integrated Acceptance And Release Requirements

Expanded morphology and syntax require maintainer-approved data/evaluation contracts built
on the accepted v0.5.0 shared and v0.5.1 nominal/reference contracts. Unavailable reference
rights or unevaluable required families block affected acceptance paths. Shared morphology,
schema and identity changes must remain consistent across nominal, verbal and contextual
recipes; separate entry points must not create independent engines or conflicting registries.

Independent integrated acceptance must trace the required inventory to executed cases,
evaluate held-out morphology and syntax separately, and exercise both deck-driven and
reading-driven journeys with valid, ambiguous and unsupported controls. Include selection,
batch continuation, blind checks, invalidation/resume, source fidelity, context preservation,
wrong-sense controls and capability reports. Real library/model checks require authorized
endpoints; native German/English import/update and presentation checks require the appropriate
environment. A morphology benchmark alone does not establish syntax accuracy.

Required-family gaps block release readiness. Publish actual coverage and limitations,
including uncertain lexemes, and carry the accepted inventory and evidence into v0.6.0.
Readiness depends on accepted v0.5.0/v0.5.1 releases and independent integrated evidence,
not a task status. Maintainer readiness acceptance and publication permission remain separate.

## Open Technical Design Checkpoints

- Design approval must specify supported subtypes and negative boundaries within the agreed broader
  clause target, translation-unit/context rules, label conventions, data/schema adapters,
  source-link ambiguity handling, selection/batch precedence and migration. The broader
  target, context-preserving units and absence of a default output-card quota are settled;
  their technical contracts remain design work, not silently delegated product policy.
- Reuse v0.5.0's evaluation design but add independently judged construction cases and
  release criteria. A morphology benchmark does not establish syntax accuracy. Capture
  required corpora/licences and available native/model environments before release checks.
- Design approval must resolve the complete morphology inventory, reference rights, schema upgrades,
  cell/recipe applicability and per-family evaluation thresholds. Missing references or
  inconclusive evaluation block affected acceptance paths; they do not satisfy coverage.
  The baseline and full-matrix reporting are requirements, not optional analyzer capabilities.
- Implementation decomposition and activation are separate work. Unresolved design
  gates block dependent tasks; this planning package does not promise selector eligibility.

## Caution

- Original Latin can admit multiple analyses; a construction name is not a unique translation.
- Smaller accepted-card yield can reflect honest uncertainty, not a defective corpus.
- Shared model errors, prompt injection and unsupported parser conventions remain risks.
- A selected passage is not permission to send unrelated deck content to a hosted provider.
- Corpus and translation licensing/provenance remain separate obligations.

## Recommendation About LLM Support

Use v0.5.0's optional, explicitly configured local/hosted pipeline and provider limits, not
a separate syntax-specific service. Include the necessary source context while enforcing
declared data boundaries; no silent endpoint fallback or authority derived from source text.
Keep library/rule/human-backed operation usable without an LLM where supported, without
claiming equivalent automatic discovery coverage. Self-reported confidence may be retained
as an estimate, never the sole acceptance threshold.

## Explicitly Excluded

- Exhaustive or infallible automatic interpretation of arbitrary historical Latin.
- Invented unattested forms, compulsory paradigm-card multiplication, or claiming full
  morphology coverage from noun nominative/genitive pairs and verb-only demonstrations.
- Accepting unsupported construction types merely because a filter or LLM requests them.
- Mandatory human approval of every automatically accepted contextual exercise.
- Invented or silently rewritten quotations presented as source-corpus examples.
- Exact-string grading of free translations or default Latin production drills.
- A second verification engine, mandatory second model, GUI, or required hosted provider.
- Direct Anki integration/provisioning (v0.6.0) or portable project bundles (v0.7.0).
