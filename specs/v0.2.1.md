# LatinitasCards v0.2.1

## Summary

Planned, inactive release. Adds deck-based phrase and grammar practice after v0.2.0's
form analysis and managed updates. Recombine reviewed vocabulary already in the learner's
Anki deck into short, natural Latin combinations, with translation and grammatical
recognition exercises. This is a bounded deck-first feature, not corpus-first generation
or a prerequisite for the v0.3.0 corpus model.

## Goals

- Practise familiar vocabulary in meaningful combinations rather than only in isolation.
- Recognize cases, numbers, agreement, and conjugated verb forms in short phrases.
- Connect grammatical form to meaning without teaching ambiguous forms as unambiguous.
- Reuse existing deck profiles, stable identities, previews, and file-export workflows.

## Potential Features

### Deck-Based Phrase And Grammar Practice

Select reviewed lexical entries and senses from an existing deck through its confirmed
profile. Use a bounded set of reviewed construction patterns to produce preposition–noun,
noun–genitive, verb–noun, and adjective–noun combinations, including short clauses where a
conjugated verb needs them. Vary supported noun cases and numbers, adjective agreement,
and verb person, number, and tense only where the lexical data and pattern support them.
Missing paradigms, uncertain senses, unsupported forms, and unsuitable combinations are
reported rather than silently guessed. Content vocabulary comes from selected deck
entries; any pattern-supplied function words are explicit in preview.

Patterns must capture case government, agreement, and relevant verb sense and valency,
not merely concatenate inflected words. A combination must be both grammatically valid
and meaningful for its selected senses. Start with reviewed, limited patterns rather
than claiming that every pair of deck words can be combined naturally. General automatic
syntax analysis remains in v0.5.0.

Illustrative combinations, when the corresponding entries are available:

| Latin | Meaning | Grammatical focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cum amico` | with a friend | `cum` governs the ablative; `amico` is ablative singular here |
| `ad amicum` | to/towards a friend | `ad` governs the accusative; `amicum` is accusative singular |
| `liber amici` | the friend's book | `amici` is genitive singular in the intended reading |
| `puella amicum videt` | the girl sees a friend | nominative subject, accusative object, third-person singular present verb |

Acceptance: reviewed synthetic deck entries generate supported combinations across
contrasting cases and verb forms. Fixtures distinguish correct case government and
agreement from a plausible but incorrect word combination, and reject unsupported forms
or incompatible senses. A learner can select vocabulary and patterns without supplying
a corpus or running an LLM.

### Translation And Form Recognition Recipes

Offer separate, selectable card recipes with one primary learning objective each:

- Latin combination to a reference translation, followed by self-assessment rather than
  exact German or English string grading.
- Latin combination to an analysis of designated forms and their contribution to the
  construction, including applicable case, number, gender, person, tense, mood, or voice.

Answers include a concise explanation of why the forms fit the combination. Translation
is available as supporting context for recognition cards. Supported inflectional variants
reuse familiar vocabulary but remain distinct exercises when the tested form or meaning
changes. Do not automatically generate every possible variant or both recipes for every
combination; save the user's vocabulary, pattern, and recipe selections and generation
limits for deterministic reruns.

Retain legitimate alternate analyses or translations. For example, `amico` in isolation
can be dative or ablative, while `cum amico` supplies evidence for ablative. `amici` can
also be nominative plural; an intended genitive reading of `liber amici` must not erase
other grammatical readings where the short context permits them. Withhold a recognition
exercise when the intended distinction cannot be taught honestly from its prompt.

Use reviewed reference content in the project's configured target language. German is
the initial/default language; English generated-content support remains a v0.4.0 feature.
The CLI control language remains English.

Acceptance: previews show complete prompts, answers, translations, explanations, and
alternate analyses where relevant. Tests contrast isolated ambiguous forms with
disambiguating combinations, and preserve ambiguity when context is insufficient.
Recipe selection and limits produce reproducible output without exact-string grading.

### Review, Identity And Export

Reuse v0.2.0's linguistic review gates and managed-update contracts. Patterns and lexical
forms need reviewed evidence; newly generated combinations and their rendered answers
require explicit approval for grammatical correctness and naturalness before export.
Unchanged approvals can be reused, but changes to the phrase, analysis, or translation
require renewed approval. Preview includes contributing deck-entry identities, selected
senses, pattern identity, form choices, and review state.

A phrase exercise is a separate coherent learning object linked to its source entries,
not a merger of those entries or a new card attached arbitrarily to one word. Persist a
stable object key for the selected lexical identities/senses, construction, and tested
form choices. Recipe and semantic exercise keys distinguish translation and recognition
cards under the existing note/card identity contracts. Wording, gloss, or theme corrections
do not by themselves reassign identity; changes to the underlying exercise require review.

Export approved selections through the deterministic Anki CSV workflow. Preserve source
notes and user-owned fields; updating or withdrawing previously exported exercises uses
managed updates rather than assuming that omission from CSV removes a note in Anki.

Acceptance: reruns preserve identities and approvals for unchanged exercises, changed
answers require renewed review, distinct tested forms do not collide, and source lexical
notes remain separate and unchanged. Export excludes unapproved candidates.

## Caution

- Morphological validity alone does not establish idiomatic or natural Latin.
- Short context cannot always distinguish case or syntactic interpretation.
- Deck entries may lack the sense, paradigm, or valency evidence a pattern needs.
- Combinatorial growth can overwhelm a deck; selection and limits are required.

## Recommendation About LLM Support

An optional, network-explicit agent may propose combinations, translations, or explanations
for review. Provider confidence is not evidence of grammatical correctness or naturalness.
The core CLI remains LLM-free, with deterministic reviewed patterns and explicit approval
before export; no provider or running model endpoint is required.

## Explicitly Excluded

- Corpus-first generation, corpus ingestion, or unrestricted sentence generation.
- Exhaustive declension/conjugation drills or coverage of every Latin construction.
- General automatic syntax-role analysis or syntax coloring.
- Arbitrary vocabulary bundles without a coherent construction and learning objective.
- German-to-Latin production, automatic translation grading, or English generated content.
- Direct integration with a running Anki instance or a required LLM service.
