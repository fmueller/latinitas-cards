## 1. Reference ownership and actual access

The maintainer must name a Latin reference-judgment owner and obtain their explicit
acceptance of the role, scope, source access and time commitment. This document's
author is not that owner. A maintainer role, existing reviewer string or test golden
does not establish an independent evaluator. Required appointments and access:

| Role | Responsibility | Current evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference owner | Latin competence for classical/biblical material; German/English meaning and terminology review, with named language specialists if needed; signs cited reference answers and permissible alternatives | Not appointed; competence/access unverified |
| Independent second judge/adjudicator | Judges a blinded overlap, resolves disagreements with citations; not the generator, acceptance checker or author of the disputed answer | Not appointed |
| Holdout custodian | Restricts answers, freezes splits and access log; can be the reference owner, not a developer tuning acceptance | Not appointed; no protected holdout exists |
| Maintainer | Approves exact protocol, permitted scope and risk policy at T-186; dispositions later failures | Approval absent; cannot replace missing judgments with sign-off alone |

Independence means solving from the task, source context and permissible references
before seeing generated answers, model/library votes, acceptance state or developer
rationale. Record conflicts of interest and shared tools/references. A library
used by the pipeline cannot also be the sole reference oracle. Different models,
prompts or judges consulting the same mistaken source are not independent truth.

## 2. Sampling frame and strata

Before drawing samples, freeze the intended supported registry, source population,
policy/checker versions and a stratum manifest. Draw source clusters first, then
tasks within them, recording inclusion probabilities, seed, exclusions and counts.
Do not select only accepted or easy outputs. Audit unsupported and failed inputs
as outcomes without adding them to a supported-claim precision denominator.

Every enabled automatic-policy cell must have a predeclared evaluation stratum.
Define cells by claim class × recipe × material characteristics below. List empty
or missing cells; do not imply the full Cartesian product has been measured.
Pooling requires reference-owner rationale and maintainer approval before holdout
is opened.

| Axis | Required coverage / discriminating controls |
| --- | --- |
| Material and input | Classical and biblical vocabulary/context; deck-derived forms, confirmed PPP mappings, controlled phrase inputs, supported corpus-derived facts and curated inputs; CSV and Anki-package extraction where supported. No original-prose syntax discovery |
| Assertion class | Label, segmentation, translation and each factual explanation clause (contract sections 3–4); distinguish underlying linguistic classes from controlled-phrase aggregate correctness |
| Recipe | Form/PPP translation and recognition; opt-in verb/noun/adjective production and form/phrase clozes; phrase translation/recognition; curated application, formation-rule recall, component explanation and mnemonic recall |
| Language and features | German and English separately; person/number/tense/mood/voice, case/number/gender, government/valency, regular and supported exceptional/stem-change cells; macron/mapping/context quality |
| Evidence path | Library/rule-only, model-assisted same-model and different-model paths if enabled; human and mixed cards separately, never pooled into automatic precision |
| Ambiguity and counterexamples | Syncretic isolated forms; multiple senses/translations; defensible segmentation conventions; wrong government/agreement; missing PPP versus supine/unconfirmed fourth part; underspecified targets; unsupported explanatory clause; agreeing wrong outputs/open objections; stale/contradictory evidence |

Curated material tests approval enforcement and reference/rendered correctness,
not automatic mnemonic acceptance. Automatic precision concerns only automatic
assertions/cards. Human approvals remain mandatory for curated content as specified.
Count mixed-card errors by the origin of the offending assertion, and also score
the complete card; never let a human sibling hide an automatic error.

Use two separately reported panels: a probability-sampled deployment panel for
rate estimates within the declared population, and a deliberately enriched challenge
panel for known counterexamples and invariants. Do not merge their rates or call
challenge success population accuracy. Retain known counterexamples permanently;
exposed examples belong to development, with new protected analogues for holdout.
Material outside evaluated cells remains processable with limitations; unresolved
candidates are withheld individually, not rejected by corpus/course identity.

## 3. Development, holdout and adjudication

1. The custodian groups related source entries/passages, lemma/paradigm families,
   translations/paraphrases, near duplicates and generated recipe siblings into
   clusters. Keep a cluster wholly in development or holdout. Record any unavoidable
   shared grammar/reference resources and contamination limits, including unknown
   model training exposure; this is not a claim of training-data independence.
2. Reference judges independently solve the task using cited permissible references,
   recording required propositions, defensible readings, segmentation convention,
   applicability/caveats, answerability and known wrong answers with reasons.
   Freeze the reference set before candidate outputs are scored. Keep initial
   judgments, disagreements, adjudicator/citations and final status; majority vote
   is not adjudication. Unresolved reference disputes are unscorable, not correct.
3. On development only, pilot the rubric and estimate eligible/accepted yield,
   cluster correlation, costs, adjudication disagreement and missing-reference
   rates. Set sample/threshold policy from risk requirements and those observations.
   No such pilot has run in T-185. Design examples from the spec are not labels.
4. Freeze code, data/split manifests, prompt/model deployments, analyzer, checker,
   comparison policy, recipes, output languages and approved criteria before one
   held-out run. Restrict answer access to judges/custodian; log all exposure.
5. Judges score blinded candidate assertions against frozen reference propositions,
   not exact answer strings. Check prompt presuppositions, every answer clause,
   examples/caveats and answer leakage. Legitimate alternatives can pass; omission
   of a material reading or an unanswerable distinction cannot silently pass.
6. Corrections after exposure preserve the original result. A fix returns to
   development and requires fresh protected affected clusters and new approval;
   do not tune/retry on heldout answers or erase failures by relabeling them.

## 4. Units, denominators and uncertainty

Record stable source/cluster/task/assertion IDs and contract Binding/Versions,
decision origin/state, card references, selection outcome and reference status.
Report every stratum and panel, including empty cells, with raw counts first:

- Source work: selected, attempted and deferred source units; attempted units with
  processing failure, and not-generated units by reason. Failures/deferrals are
  not linguistic rejections. Do not estimate undiscovered candidate totals.
- Distinct automatic assertions: accepted A, independently scorable accepted J,
  erroneous accepted E, and unscorable accepted U = A − J. Count reused assertions
  once; card appearances are a separate denominator. Strict precision is (J − E)/J
  conditional on scorability, explicitly not a success claim for U. Also report
  conservative correct fraction (J − E)/A when A > 0, counting U as unproven.
  Any U blocks a pass for that cell under this proposed protocol.
- Known candidate tasks: generated G, acceptance-assessed D, acceptance-withheld W
  by reason, processing-failed/deferred candidates separately, and accepted tasks
  K. Report K/G (observed candidate yield) and K/D (acceptance coverage), naming
  denominators and overlaps. W/D describes assessed withholding. Candidates with
  incomplete prerequisite checks are not completed acceptance assessments.
- Complete cards: all eligible cards before suppression/output selection, wholly
  automatic, human and mixed separately; any-claim-error cards / judged cards;
  accepted-unselected and exported counts/reasons. Output limits cannot improve
  preselection precision by hiding eligible errors. Report shared-claim multiplicity.

If A or J is zero, precision is not evaluable, never 100%. If G or D is zero, or
source enumeration is missing, the corresponding coverage is not evaluable.
Preserve numerators and denominators; do not replace unevaluable rates with zero.
Reference disputes and missing judgments are evidence gaps, not processing failures
or withheld claims.
Report E/A alongside U/A so missing labels cannot disappear from error reporting.

For independent sampled clusters, use an exact one-sided binomial error bound on
the cluster event “at least one erroneous automatically accepted assertion/card”.
Select one predeclared primary task per sampled cluster for a binomial task-rate
analysis; do not treat many sibling forms/cards as independent observations.
Report assertion/card rates descriptively and cluster-aware uncertainty for them
under a predeclared method approved with statistical review. If too few clusters
support that method, report insufficient evidence, not a narrow IID interval.
Do not treat the cluster event bound as an assertion-level precision bound.
Shared library rules/model families can cause cross-cluster errors: show error
concentrations by those dependencies, independent reference adjudication, and
systematic wrong-answer challenge results. Independence cannot be proved by prompt
separation. If the sampling assumptions fail, the confidence claim cannot pass.

## 5. Sample-size and pass/fail policy: unresolved parameters

Method source: [NIST/SEMATECH, binomial confidence intervals](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm),
read 2026-11-12, recommends exact binomial treatment for small samples/few failures
and describes one-sided intervals. It supplies a statistical method, not an
acceptable Latin learning-card error rate or a justified population model.

For each enabled stratum s, the maintainer and reference owner must predeclare
maximum error rate εs, confidence-tail allocation αs, primary metric/unit, minimum
independent clusters, minimum accepted/judged tasks, treatment of severe errors,
and any coverage floor with intended population/recipe justification. Account for
multiple strata by preallocating a family error budget (for example αs whose sum
does not exceed the approved total), not testing many cells at an undisclosed level.
No values for these parameters are approved or justified by this pass.

For zero errors in n independent Bernoulli units, the one-sided upper error bound
is 1 − αs^(1/n), derived from P(zero errors) = (1 − p)^n. Thus a proposed design
minimum is ceil(log(αs)/log(1 − εs)), conditional on that model and zero errors.
Nonzero errors require the exact binomial-tail inversion, not the zero-error
formula. Accepted-task yield and clustering require additional source units;
development pilot evidence must justify that acquisition budget. This is a
conditional sizing method, **not a numerical minimum or evidence of feasibility**.
Without Latin-specific risk requirements, pilot yield/correlation or owner-approved
thresholds, this pass cannot justify numerical minima or pass thresholds.

Proposed decision rule per cell after parameters are justified and approved:

- PASS only with permitted, uncontaminated independent references, frozen scope,
  satisfied minimum sizes, approved uncertainty bound ≤ εs for the declared metric,
  no unscorable accepted items, all required negative controls passing, and any
  separately approved severity/coverage criteria met. Report coverage even if no
  floor is approved. A cluster pass alone does not pass assertion/card precision.
- FAIL for a violated approved bound/criterion or an accepted prohibited control
  (unsupported/stale/open-objection/approval-bypass). Record the actual failure;
  low yield cannot excuse it. A low point estimate alone cannot establish PASS.
- INSUFFICIENT for absent parameters/rights/judges, contamination, zero acceptance,
  inadequate sample/cluster count, unresolved reference disputes or invalid uncertainty
  assumptions. Report observed failures even alongside insufficiency. Never substitute
  a model vote, fixture result or “no observed errors” for missing evidence.
